LAME (MP3 encoder)
------------------
GPW Uploader runs LAME as a separate program (lame.exe, installed next to
the app) to convert WAV masters to MP3. It is not linked into the app.

  https://sourceforge.net/projects/lame/files/lame/3.100/
  For the exact source of the lame.exe shipped with this app, contact
  GPW support through https://ghostproducerworld.com — it will be provided
  for at least three years from the date you received this version.

You may replace lame.exe with your own build of LAME; the app calls it as
"lame --cbr -b 320 -q 0 <input.wav> <output.mp3>".
